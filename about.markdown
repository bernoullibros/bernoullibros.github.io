We are Bernoulli Bros, a STEM racing UK team (formerly F1 in Schools UK) from Reading. Driven by a passion for aerodynamics, engineering excellence, and competitive motorsport, we aim to push the limits of speed.

### Contact us
We are actively looking for forward-thinking sponsors, technical mentors, and academic partners to power our campaign. By supporting Bernoulli Bros, you invest directly in young technical talent steering toward the future of UK engineering. Contact us [here](mailto:team@bernoullibros.co.uk).
