What is STEM Racing UK?

STEM Racing is chance for teams to design, analyse, build, and test miniature F1 models. These models will be powered by CO2 cartridges and teams must find ways to make an aerodynamic car. Not only will this competition test teams’ engineering ability but also their brand identity, ability to find sponsors and project management skills. Bernoulli Bros will be competing in the regional finals - London West in February as a part of the development class.
